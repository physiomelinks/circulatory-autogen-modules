i_M g_M: where it comes from

i_M Argus2026_v01, instance davis2020_wistar. g_M = A_mem rho_M gamma_M, with rho_M calibrated by libcuflynx to one obs_data item. Rebuilt by venv/bin/python tools/i_M_davis2020_kinetics.py gm-report.

1. The source

Davis et al. 2020, Hypertension 76:1915, Fig. 3C (the PDF raster, 200 ppi). Left: representative raw deactivation traces. Right: the XE-991-sensitive M-current density per cell. Drawn on it, in orange: the 11 Wistar points as digitised (open circles), their median (-4.01 pA/pF, bar) and IQR (-4.58 to -3.20, box). In blue: Davis's stated median -4.032 pA/pF. The plotted black bars match the mean +- SEM of the points (-3.90 +- 0.34), not the median +- IQR the legend states.

Davis 2021 DPhil thesis, Ch. 4, pp. 172-173 (the same data, Fig. 4.10C). It defines the measure: held at -25 mV, a 1 s step to -55 mV, "the difference in current following the initial ohmic change, and the steady-state current towards the end of the step", of the 10 uM XE-991-sensitive current. So the measure is the deactivation relaxation, not the steady-state difference between -25 and -55 mV.

  • Operation. mean_in_range_minus_initial(i_M, start_frac 0.9, end_frac 1.0) on sub-experiment 1. It takes the mean of i_M over the last 10 % of the step minus its first sample. The gate w is continuous and V jumps, so the first sample is the current just after the ohmic change. This is an existing libcuflynx operation.
  • Value. -4.032 pA/pF (Wistar median, n = 11) x Cm 30 pF = -120.96 pA = -0.12096 nA. The sign is negative because the outward current falls during the step.
  • std. The SEM of the digitised points, 0.34 pA/pF, x 30 pF = 0.0102 nA. Davis report a median +- IQR, with no SEM.
Assumptions. (1) Cm = 30 pF (the SN model's soma capacitance): Davis report densities only, so g_M scales with this choice. (2) The perforated-patch liquid junction potential of 24.3 mV is not corrected: Davis 2019 bioRxiv Methods say it was not corrected, so the true potentials may differ from the nominal -25 and -55 mV. The protocol uses the nominal values, as the source does. (3) Protocol voltages. The bioRxiv preprint's legend says -20 to -50 mV; the published paper and the thesis say -25 to -55 mV. The published values are used. (4) Only the channel is modelled. Davis's XE-991 subtraction removes the other currents, so the module alone is the right comparison.

CVODE simulation of the module, through the obs_data protocol. Data: -121.0 pA. At a given g_M, the relaxation is 0.50 of the total change from the holding current to the end of the step. (The first fit read the data as the change between the two true steady states, w_inf(-55)(-55 - E_K) - w_inf(-25)(-25 - E_K) = -47.3 mV x g_M, which gives -121 pA at 0.002556 uS by construction; within the 1 s step it reaches the value in the last column.) The steady-state difference also includes the instantaneous ohmic drop of the M current (w(-25) times the 30 mV step), and Davis exclude that drop. Reading the data as a steady-state difference (the first fit, 0.002556 uS) therefore gives a g_M 2.14x smaller than the relaxation reading. The old default 0.00263 uS was a hand calculation of the same steady-state kind with E_K -93.7 mV.

Dependence on tau_w. The reading is taken within Davis's 1 s step, so it depends on how far the relaxation has gone. With the present tau_w (466 ms at -55 mV) it is 87 % complete, and g_M = 0.005479 uS; with the refitted tau_w of instance davis2020_wistar_kinetics (tau_w_num 2.36e-10, tau_w_min 104.8 ms) the relaxation is complete within the step and g_M would be 0.004765 uS. If tau_w changes, recalibrate this instance.
